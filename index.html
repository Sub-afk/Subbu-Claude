Who I am

About me

I'm Subbaiah, a poet, writer and singer based in Bangalore. I have been writing for as long as I can remember. I finished my first book when I was 10 years old.

My debut poetry collection, From Reverie to Requiem, came out in 2025 and has sold over 600 copies. I sing with the band Ek Bun Samosa, and most nights end with a couple of games of Valorant.

By day I'm a lawyer. By night, I write.

From Reverie to Requiem

22 poems. Whimsy on one side, heartbreak on the other.

The collection travels the whole arc of feeling. It starts with playful poems about pets and eccentric characters, moves through family, faith and love, and arrives at grief and the weight of reality. Like its cover, it lives half in light and half in dark.

A collection not just to be read, but to be felt.

The writer

Since the age of 10

Call me a writer in the making, although the making started early. I wrote my first book when I was 10, and I have not really stopped since. From Reverie to Requiem is the first to reach readers. It will not be the last.
